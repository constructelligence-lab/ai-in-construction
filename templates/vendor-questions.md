# Vendor questions checklist

Print this, take it into the call, and write the answers down. Take the same list to every vendor so you can
compare like with like, and keep the answers for your week-12 decision.

## Fit and outcomes

- [ ] Which specific decision does this improve, and who inside a contractor normally owns that decision?
- [ ] How would we know in twelve weeks whether it worked?
- [ ] What do customers usually find hardest in the first month?
- [ ] What do you not do well, or not do at all yet?

## How it actually works

- [ ] What data does it need, and what happens when a piece of it is missing or stale?
- [ ] Can we demo this on two of our own projects — one clean, one messy — rather than your sample data?
- [ ] Show me how a wrong answer gets corrected, and whether the correction is retained.
- [ ] Can I see the rows, sources or assumptions behind every number?
- [ ] What is your accuracy claim, how was it measured, and on how many projects like ours?
- [ ] What is the failure mode we should expect, and how would we notice it?

## Integration and access

- [ ] What exactly do you read, and by which mechanism: API, read-only database connection, or file export?
- [ ] Do you ever require write access to any of our systems? (The answer we want is no.)
- [ ] How long does implementation take, and how many hours of our people does it need?
- [ ] Who owns the mapping of our cost codes, job structure and job names — you or us?
- [ ] What happens when a job is renamed, a company changes, or a cost code is added?

## Data, security and legal

- [ ] Where is our data stored and processed, and which subprocessors are involved?
- [ ] Is our data used to train models — yours or anyone else's — including subprocessors? In writing, please.
- [ ] What is the retention period, and what happens at termination: export format, deletion, confirmation?
- [ ] Do you support SSO, MFA, role-based access and audit logs on the tier we are buying?
- [ ] Which certifications do you hold (SOC 2, ISO 27001), and can we see the latest report — not the badge?
- [ ] How do you notify us of a security incident, and how quickly?
- [ ] What insurance do you carry, and what is your liability capped at?
- [ ] Can our client's data and imagery legally go into this? [Check against project requirements.]

## Commercials

- [ ] What is the total cost in year one, including implementation, and what is the cost in year two?
- [ ] Which pricing model: per seat, per project, per volume, or platform plus modules?
- [ ] What are the overage charges if we have a busy quarter?
- [ ] What is the notice period, and what does it cost to leave?
- [ ] If we stop, what do we keep — our data, our history, our configuration?

## References

- [ ] Two references on jobs similar in size, trade and type to ours. May we call them without you on the call?
- [ ] One reference that stopped using it, or reduced usage — what happened?

## Red flags

- [ ] "95% accuracy" with no definition of accuracy or dataset
- [ ] Cannot show the working behind a number
- [ ] Wants write access to the ERP or the schedule
- [ ] Only demos on their own data
- [ ] Vague about training on your data, or about subprocessors
- [ ] Cannot describe what happens when their input data is wrong
- [ ] Liability capped at the fee, with no conversation about what that means for you
- [ ] Pushes a multi-year commitment before a pilot has produced evidence
